Privacy Policy for Arrow Land

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Arrow Land is a puzzle game for Android published by Pocket Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. You do not need an account to play, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Arrow Land shows ads through Google AdMob: full-screen ads between some levels, and optional reward videos you choose to watch for a hint, an undo or an extra heart. To serve and measure these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • your IP address and approximate (not precise) location derived from it
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings, or at Google Ads Settings.

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from Settings › Privacy choices in the game. Where the law of a US state gives you the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising, the same menu is used for that.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Arrow Land offers optional purchases such as Remove Ads and hint packs. All payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; the game only receives which product was bought and whether the purchase succeeded, so it can unlock it and restore it later.

Game data stored on your device

Your level progress, settings, hint count, purchases you have unlocked and the counters that keep ads spaced out are saved only on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not run analytics or tracking of our own.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, files, messages, microphone or camera data, or precise location.
  • We do not sell any data to anyone.

Data deletion

Because we do not keep any data about you on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove the game's data from your device, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. To ask Google to delete advertising data, use the controls in your Google account or Google Ads Settings. You can also email us with any request and we will help.

Children's privacy

Arrow Land is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will take steps to help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
